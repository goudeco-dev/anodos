Privacy Policy

Stenvo (beta, formerly Anodos) · Last updated: 18 November 2026

Stenvo is a workout log for the gym, made by an independent developer based in Greece. This policy explains what happens to your data when you use the app.

The short version

  • Your data stays on your phone. There is no account and no sign-in.
  • We do not collect, sell or share your personal data.
  • The app contains no ads, no analytics and no tracking.

Data stored on your phone

Everything you enter is stored only in the app's local database on your device: exercises, programs, workouts and sets, settings, and, if you choose to fill them in, your profile (sex, year of birth, height) and body-weight log. This data never leaves your phone unless you export it yourself. We cannot see it or access it.

Deleting the app deletes this data. If you want to keep a copy, use Settings → Export data first.

Exports and imports

Export creates a backup file and opens the iOS share sheet. The file goes only where you choose to send or save it (for example Files, iCloud Drive or a message). Import reads a backup file that you pick. We never receive these files.

Notifications

If you allow notifications, the app schedules a local notification on your phone when a rest period ends. These notifications are created on the device; no data is sent anywhere.

App updates (Expo)

The app checks for and downloads updates from the servers of Expo (650 Industries, Inc.), the platform it is built with. Like any internet request, these update checks include technical information such as your IP address, the platform (iOS) and the app and update version. They do not include any of your workout or profile data. See Expo's privacy policy.

TestFlight (beta testing)

During the beta, the app is distributed through Apple's TestFlight. Apple may share with us the information that TestFlight provides to developers: for example the name and email address you were invited with, how many times you opened the app, crash reports, and any feedback or screenshots you choose to send. We use this information only to improve the app and to contact you about the beta. See Apple's privacy policy.

"How to do it" videos

The "How to do it" button opens a YouTube search for the exercise in the YouTube app or in Safari. From that point YouTube's own privacy policy applies. The app sends only the exercise name in the search.

Health information

Workout, body-weight and profile data can be sensitive. Because it is stored only on your device, we never process it. Estimates shown in the app (such as estimated one-rep max or strength level) are for information only and are not medical advice.

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13.

Your rights

Since we do not hold your personal data, there is nothing for us to access, correct or delete on our side; you control your data in the app. For anything we receive through TestFlight (such as feedback), you can ask us to access or delete it by contacting us at the address below. You also have the right to complain to your data protection authority (in Greece, the Hellenic Data Protection Authority).

Changes

If the app changes how it handles data (for example when optional accounts are added), we will update this page and the date above before the change reaches you.
